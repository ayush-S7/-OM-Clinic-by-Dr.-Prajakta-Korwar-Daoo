Privacy policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

This policy explains how this website, for the clinic of Dr. Prajakta Korwar Daoo, handles your information. It covers this website only, not your care at the clinic.

What this website collects

This website does not collect, store or sell personal information. It has no user accounts, no database, no analytics and no advertising trackers, and it sets no cookies of its own. It remembers your light or dark mode choice in your browser only, and that choice is never sent anywhere.

The appointment form

When you submit the booking form, your name, concern, preferred time and any notes are put into a WhatsApp message. Your browser then opens WhatsApp so you can review and send it to the clinic’s number. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp, and this website does not receive a copy.

Once you send the message, it is handled by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy, and the clinic will see your name and phone number. Please do not include detailed medical history in the form. Share that during your consultation.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts: the page loads its typeface from Google, which means your browser contacts Google’s servers and your IP address is shared with them.
  • Google Maps: the location map on this page is loaded from Google, so Google receives your IP address and may use cookies when the map loads. The “Get directions” link also opens Google Maps. Google’s privacy policy applies to both.
  • Phone and WhatsApp links: these open your phone or WhatsApp app.

Reviews

The patient reviews on this site were originally posted publicly on Google. If you wrote one and want it removed or your name changed, contact the clinic and it will be updated.

Your choices

You can contact the clinic by phone instead of using the form. If you have given the clinic information and want to ask about it, call 074980 09568 or visit using the address above.

Changes to this policy

If this website changes in a way that affects your information, such as adding analytics, this section will be updated and the date above will change.
